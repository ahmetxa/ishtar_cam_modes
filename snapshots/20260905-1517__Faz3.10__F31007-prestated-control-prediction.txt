WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
---------------------
Faz3.10 section 8 grades the negative control on its FALSIFIABILITY, not on its
result: "A control whose prediction is absent, or written after the capture,
does not satisfy this criterion whatever the capture showed." So the prediction
is committed here, in advance, in a form that can fail.

  pref_camera_resolution left at "full", setting pref_48m_key = 1 and restarting
  AGC will derive:
        pref_camera_picturesize_back_key   = 8192x6144

--------------------------------------
